MORE THAN A TROPHY

A Competition That Changed Football

The European Champion Clubs' Cup began in 1955–56, bringing national champions together to compete for continental glory. In 1992–93, the competition entered a new era under the UEFA Champions League name. Over seven decades, different generations of players, managers and clubs have created a history filled with triumph, heartbreak and extraordinary comebacks.
